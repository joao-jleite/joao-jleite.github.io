Client project

Dermatech Medical: a website in three languages

Brazilian distributor of skin and scalp imaging analyzers for aesthetic, dermatology and hair clinics.

Home page, English version. Hero image from the client's own mockup. Equipment overview, English version. Equipment artwork supplied by the client. The same scalp area under three light spectra (Portuguese version). Scalp images from the manufacturer's catalog. Home page on a phone (Portuguese version). Hero image from the client's own mockup. Contact form on a phone: it opens WhatsApp with the message already written (Portuguese version).

I built and published the site from the client's page structure and visual direction: layout, front-end, the three-language setup, lead routing and go-live.

  • 9 public pages in Portuguese, English and Spanish. The site remembers the visitor's language choice, and on desktop the switch changes the language in place, without reloading.
  • Every WhatsApp button rotates between the company's two sales consultants, and the contact form opens WhatsApp with the message already written.
  • Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no framework and no build step, so the client can host it anywhere and edit it later without a developer toolchain.
  • Moved the DNS to Cloudflare without disrupting company email, and set up the sitemap, structured data and Google Search Console.

Client project

D’Andrade Moda: an online boutique with an admin panel

Small Brazilian boutique of modest, timeless women's fashion.

Home page (Portuguese). Category menu on the left, WhatsApp support always one tap away. Featured items: prices, color and photo badges, favorites. Product photos by the client. Product page: color, size and quantity come from the stock the owner keeps in the admin panel. On a phone: search at the top and an app-style bottom bar. Product page on a phone, with the photo gallery.

I built, launched and handed over the store: storefront, admin panel, database and the move to the client's own account and domain.

  • Catalog by category with colors, sizes and quantities, search and favorites. The bag is saved in the browser and becomes a complete order summary sent on WhatsApp.
  • Private admin panel where the owner adds items and photos and updates prices and stock herself. Every admin screen and API route checks the session on the server.
  • React and TypeScript on Cloudflare Workers, with a D1 (SQLite) database: no server to maintain.
  • Moved the data, the site and the domain from my account to the client's own Cloudflare account, checking every table after the copy.
  • React

  • D1
  • Tailwind
